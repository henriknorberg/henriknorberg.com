01 / About

CAVE
YOGI.

Henrik Norberg is a meditation teacher, cave yogi, wanderer, and mind explorer.

Henrik is a certified teacher of The Mind Illuminated (TMI). He was personally trained and instructed by Culadasa (John Yates) at Dharma Treasure Retreat Center.

His practice is particularly interested in pre-sectarian Buddhism, samadhi, concentration, jhana, insight meditation, and the direct investigation of experience.

02 / Practice

TRAIN
THE MIND.

01

The Mind Illuminated

A systematic approach to developing stable attention, overcoming distraction, and progressing through the stages of meditation practice.

02

Samadhi

Concentration, unification of mind, pleasure, stability, and the conditions that allow deeper meditative states to emerge.

03

Jhana

Exploring the absorption practices described in early Buddhist traditions alongside contemporary approaches to meditation.

04

Insight

Using a concentrated mind to investigate experience directly: impermanence, craving, identification, and the construction of self.

03 / Wilderness Dharma

DHARMA
IN THE WILD.

Sometimes the fastest way to see the mind is to remove everything that normally distracts it.

Caves, mountains, deserts, forests, solitude and long periods without screens create a different context for meditation. Wilderness practice is not an escape from Dharma. It is a laboratory for investigating the mind.
